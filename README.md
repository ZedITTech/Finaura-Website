# Finaura

Finaura is a personal finance app built around one stubborn idea: you shouldn't have to hand your bank login to a stranger just to figure out where your money went this month.

No linked accounts. No screen-scraping your checking account. No data broker quietly profiting off your grocery habits. You put in what you spend, Finaura helps you make sense of it. That's the whole deal.

## Why this exists

I didn't set out to build a finance app. I set out to stop losing my mind every Sunday night reconciling a spreadsheet that had grown about fourteen tabs too many. What started as "add one more column" eventually turned into "delete the spreadsheet and build the thing I actually wanted."

A few things pushed me over the edge:

- Every budgeting app worth using wanted a subscription, and most of them wanted a subscription *and* my bank credentials. That combination never sat right with me.
- The apps that didn't require a login were usually clunky enough that I'd abandon them within a week, right back to the spreadsheet.
- I didn't actually want automation. I wanted to be the one entering my numbers — turns out the fact of typing in a purchase is half of what keeps me honest about it. Auto-sync quietly removes the one part of budgeting that actually works.

So Finaura is deliberately manual. That's not a limitation I haven't gotten around to fixing — it's the point. You log your own transactions, and in exchange nothing about your finances ever needs to touch a third-party bank integration.

## What it actually does

At its core, Finaura is a place to track what you spend, set budgets that reflect how you actually live, and see the patterns that a bank statement alone never shows you. It's not trying to be a robo-advisor or a trading platform or a "financial wellness ecosystem." It's a tool for people who want a clear, honest picture of their own money without giving up control of it.

## Where things stand

Finaura is live and stable — this isn't a prototype or a waitlist page. It's the app I use myself, every week, which is honestly the best quality bar I know how to hold it to. It'll keep evolving, but nothing here is held together with tape.

## A note on scope

If you're curious what it actually does day to day, the best way to find out is to use it.
